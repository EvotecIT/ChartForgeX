The chart look

The default visual language for every ChartForgeX chart in the Graphite look: frame, axes, marks, colour, legends, labels, and the families the audit flagged. Each chart below is drawn as ChartForgeX should draw it; the SVG follows the rules on this page, so Codex can port the rules and compare renders against these.

01

Principles

  • Flat. No gradients, gloss, top highlights, inner shadows or glass in the default theme. Depth comes from layout, not effects. Effect styles stay available as opt-in themes.
  • One frame. A chart draws one surface at most, and none when the host already provides a card. Never a plot card inside a chart card.
  • Data first, ink last. Gridlines are faint and horizontal only; axes have no ticks unless a scale needs them; legends and labels are small and muted; marks carry the colour.
  • Status colours mean status. Red, amber and green are reserved for state. Ordinary series use the categorical palette. A series named for a state uses that state's colour, and the healthy one is drawn quiet.
  • Same in SVG and PNG. Every rule here applies to both renderers and both themes.

  • Title 17 px bold (700, never black/heavy), subtitle 13.5 px muted, both left-aligned to the plot.
  • Legend directly under the subtitle, left-aligned, inline; 10 px square swatches (14 × 2 px line for line series), 6 px to the label, 16 px between items. Hidden when there is one series.
  • Plot has no background and no border. Horizontal gridlines only, 1 px in the grid token; the zero baseline uses the axis token.
  • Axes: no y-axis line and no ticks; y labels 12 px muted, right-aligned 8 px left of the plot; x labels centred under categories. Axis titles only when the unit is not in the title or subtitle, written horizontally above the y labels, never rotated.

  • Lines 2 px, straight by default, round joins; no markers except the last point (and hover). Area fills are flat at 12 % opacity, only for single-series areas.
03

Marks and spacing

  • Bars: flat fill; 2 px corner radius on the value end only (top of a column, right of a bar), square at the baseline. Category band padding 32 % outer; 2 px between bars in a group; stacked segments separated by a 1 px surface-coloured gap; no outlines.
  • Value labels on bars sit just outside the value end in 12 px text-2, inside only when they fit with ≥ 4.5:1 contrast. Compact numbers (1.2k, 3.4M) with the full value in the accessible name and tooltip.
  • Points: scatter markers 6 px with a 1.5 px surface outline; bubbles flat at 70 % opacity with a 1 px surface outline.
  • Plot inset: 8 px between the plot and its labels; the chart's outer padding is 16 × 18 px when ChartForgeX draws a frame, 0 when the host owns the card.
  • Small multiples and dashboards: panels sit on the page surface separated by 16 px gaps; a panel is a chart with its own title, not a card inside a card; panel titles 15 px bold.
04

Colour

Defaults for the Graphite theme. All of them are tokens, overridable by themes and brand kits as today.

RoleSwatchesRule
categorical
Blue, teal, orange, violet, pink, sky, olive, slate, in that order. The first three stay distinguishable for the common colour-vision deficiencies; beyond eight series, group into "Other".
state
Danger, warning, info, quiet healthy, neutral. Combine with patterns where state must survive greyscale print (existing pattern support).
sequential
Single-hue blue, lightness monotonic, for counts and magnitudes. Zero counts use the neutral cell colour, not the first step (D24).
diverging
Orange negative arm, neutral centre, blue positive arm (D24). Blue–orange stays readable for red–green deficiencies.
  • Checks in tests: adjacent categorical colours keep ΔE ≥ 20 under simulated deuteranopia and protanopia for the first three, ≥ 12 for the rest; sequential steps are monotonic in lightness; text on any fill meets 4.5:1.
  • Dark theme uses the lifted variants (shown when the page is dark), not the light colours on a dark surface.
  • Healthy is quiet: a "good" state in a chart (passed checks, healthy hours) uses the quiet or neutral token, never full green, unless the chart is about health alone.

Part-to-whole: donut and pie

Findings by control area
Share of 1,284 findings
  • Donut by default (pie on request): inner radius 62 %; 2 px surface-coloured gaps between slices; slices ordered largest first from 12 o'clock clockwise; at most six slices, the rest grouped as "Other" in neutral.
  • Centre shows the total in 20 px bold with a 12 px muted caption.
  • Legend becomes a list to the right (below on narrow): swatch, name, value right-aligned, percentage in muted. Name and value stay in the same row, so the audit's detached value columns disappear.
  • No labels on slices by default; slice labels with leaders only when the host asks and they fit (collision service).

  • Gauge: 240° arc, 14 px stroke, butt ends; track in neutral-2; optional bands (for example 0–60 danger, 60–80 warning, 80–100 quiet) drawn as a thin 4 px outer ring, not as the main arc; the value arc in the first series colour, switching to the warning or danger colour only when the value falls in that band. No legend.
  • Bullet: flat rectangular qualitative bands in three neutral steps (no capsules, no colour per row), a measure bar one third of the band height in text colour, a 2 px target line one band tall, the value right of the band in 12.5 px bold followed by the target in muted; labels left; a shared axis at the bottom. Rows below target show only the value in the danger colour (no red bars, no separate dot), so a page of bullets stays calm. No legend.
  • Linear gauge (new) follows the bullet anatomy with one row and a value marker triangle above the band.

  • Sankey: nodes are 10 px wide neutral bars; links are flat at 35 % opacity in the source node's colour (categorical for sources, state colours for state nodes); node labels outside the node with the value in bold after the name; links ordered to minimise crossings; no legend (nodes are labelled).
  • Funnel: centred flat bars, one colour (first series) with steps lighter by stage, 2 px gaps; labels inside or beside: stage name, value, and percentage of the first stage in muted. No gradients, no 3D cone unless requested.
  • Heatmap: square-ish cells with 2 px gaps and 2 px radius; sequential ramp; zero cells neutral; row and column labels muted 12 px; a compact ramp legend (5 steps with min and max) under the plot instead of a series legend.

09

Acceptance for the implementation

  • Graphite (light and dark) is the default theme and follows every rule above in SVG and PNG; the existing effect styles remain available as named opt-in themes, documented in the migration notes.
  • The ChartForgeX gallery regenerated in both themes matches this page for the families shown; every other family follows the same frame, axis, legend, colour and label rules.
  • Colour tests: categorical CVD separation, monotonic sequential lightness, text contrast on fills, and that no default series colour equals a state colour.
  • Visual baselines are updated with a before/after record; the SVG↔PNG baseline still passes.
